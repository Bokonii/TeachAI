Privacy

How your data is handled

Local first
  • Accounts, chats, groups, and reminders are stored in a local SQLite database.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never as plain text.
  • When cloud AI is connected, the active conversation is sent to your chosen provider to generate a reply.
  • Your API key is only sent to that provider — never to any other service.
